About TSA

Hi judges! Welcome to our 2025-2026 TSA Webmasters Submission. It's a pleasure to host you, and we appreciate your time and consideration.

TSA has always been an inspirational force, that pushes our creativity and encourages us to make a real impact in our community and beyond. Through engaging projects like this community resource hub, we wanted to create something that connects people with volunteer opportunities, local drives, and other opportunities, helping residents experience life from a brighter perspective. This initiative not only helps individuals find newer ways to meaningfully connect with others, but also fosters a culture of service, collaboration, and leadership within our chapter. By connecting people with meaningful opportunities, we've see firsthand how technology can be a powerful tool for positive change.

On this page, you'll find all our official TSA documentation, formal citations for all resources, and a list of all the tools used in our website.

Thank you!
