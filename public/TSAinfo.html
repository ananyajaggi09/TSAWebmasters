About TSA

Our TSA chapter inspires us to make a real impact in our community and beyond. Through engaging projects, like this community resource hub, we connect people with volunteer opportunities, local drives, and other ways to give back. This initiative not only helps individuals find ways to contribute, but also fosters a culture of service, collaboration, and leadership within our chapter. By connecting people with meaningful opportunities, we see firsthand how technology can be a powerful tool for positive change.
